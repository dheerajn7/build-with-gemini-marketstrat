# My agent: MarketStrat (Corporate Strategy & Equity Intelligence Agent)
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps corporate strategists and investors evaluate company financial health and market perception with a catalog of tracked companies, analyst reports, and strategic recommendations.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User's tracked companies / watchlist, investment/business thesis preferences, risk tolerance, and key focus metrics (e.g., margins, growth, capital efficiency).
- Tools: Look up company financial fundamentals (P&L, growth, margin trends), fetch analyst consensus/ratings, retrieve recent stock performance, and generate strategic recommendations.
- Catalog/UI: Catalog of tracked public/private companies, financial scorecard comparison cards, and strategic action item lists rendered via A2UI.
- Image gen: Strategic visual summaries (e.g., stylized SWOT analysis diagrams, market positioning matrix / quadrant graphics).
- Sandbox: Code sandbox for computing financial metrics (CAGR, EBITDA margins, multiples) and calculating correlations between sentiment/ratings and stock price movements.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for financial ratio & correlation calculations, RAG Engine for grounding on earnings call transcripts / 10-K filings.
